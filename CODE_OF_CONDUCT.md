# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome different experiences, explain disagreements with evidence, and protect personal and health information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, limit participation, or exclude participants when necessary. Apply decisions consistently and proportionately. Reports and appeals can be sent privately to the [repository maintainer](https://github.com/laiflonglearner) using the contact details on their profile. Security vulnerabilities belong in the private reporting channel described in SECURITY.md.
